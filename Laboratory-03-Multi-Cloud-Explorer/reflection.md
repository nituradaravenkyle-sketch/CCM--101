# Checkpoint 8 – Mission Reflection

## Mission Reflection

Throughout this mission, the cloud platform that impressed me the most was Amazon Web Services (AWS). I was impressed by the wide range of services that AWS provides for different computing, storage, networking, and database requirements. Its flexibility and scalability make it useful for both small startups and large enterprises.

AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP) have many similarities. All three provide cloud-based computing, storage, databases, networking, identity management, and other services. They also allow organizations to scale their resources based on their needs and provide services that can support applications, websites, data analytics, and enterprise systems.

Business requirements have an important influence on cloud platform selection. Organizations need to consider their budget, existing technology, performance requirements, security, scalability, location, and the skills of their IT staff. For example, a company that heavily uses Microsoft products may benefit from Azure, while an organization focused on artificial intelligence and machine learning may prefer GCP.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. I learned that the best choice depends on the client's specific requirements and business goals. Comparing equivalent services and evaluating different cloud providers helped me understand how cloud solutions can be matched to real-world problems.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has also improved after completing this mission. I added research about AWS, Azure, and GCP, comparison tables, client recommendations, decision-making information, and a Linux server investigation. This mission helped me organize my work more professionally and gave me a better understanding of how cloud computing concepts can be applied in practical situations.
